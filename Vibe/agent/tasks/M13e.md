## 1. The three rulings

### 1.1 🔧 You were right — my `>=` fix was necessary but not sufficient. Finish it.

You reported that `AttemptAsync`'s `race.IsSettled` short-circuit drops the same-`gen` re-announce when a
link from that epoch is already up — **exactly the post-`adb tcpip 5555` case the ruling existed to
enable.** Correct, and you were right to flag rather than decide it: it means deciding when a live link
may be torn down.

**The ruling, now in `PROTOCOL.md`:**

- **Compare the announced endpoint against the currently-connected one.**
- **Different → disconnect and redial.** That is promotion, and it is the entire point.
- **Identical and already verified → ignore, idempotently.** No thrash, no reconnect storm.
- **Only act on `verified: true`.**
- **Rate-limit redials** (pick a floor, state it) so a flapping phone cannot thrash the link.

**Put the decision in a pure static** — something like
`ShouldRedial(announcedEndpoint, currentEndpoint, verified, sinceLastRedial) → bool` — and drive
`hotspotsim` through it by name, including the promotion case (`:37419` live, `:5555` announced at the
same `gen` → redial) and the idempotent case (same endpoint → no redial).

### 1.2 ✅ RULING: the phone's `prefer` behaviour is CORRECT. The spec was wrong, and I fixed the spec.

You reported the phone parses `prefer` and ignores it, degrading 1.5's escape hatch to "re-arm and
announce whatever `adbd` lands on".

**That is the right behaviour and it cannot be otherwise.** A non-root app **cannot** arm legacy tcpip —
that needs `service.adb.tcp.port` via `setprop`, which requires the `shell` or `root` UID.
`WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS` covers `Settings.Global`/`Secure` only, which is enough for `adb_wifi_enabled`
and nothing more. **My §1.5 wording implied a capability Android does not grant.**

`PROTOCOL.md` now says `prefer` is **advisory**, and that **the desktop performs promotion itself** with
`adb tcpip 5555` once it holds any working ADB connection.

**Work required — small:**
- Phone side: **no behaviour change.** Add a comment at the parse site recording *why* `prefer` is
  advisory (the `setprop` UID constraint), so a future session does not "fix" it. Log the received value
  at debug level and move on.
- Desktop side: confirm the promotion call (`adb tcpip 5555` after a verified connection) exists and
  fires. **If M13c left it unwired, wire it** — it is now the only route to a stable port. Report which.
