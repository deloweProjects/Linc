## 2. 🔴 PART B — make the Android app beautiful

**This is the owner's oldest outstanding complaint: *"the android app is very ugly."*** It is a
**presentation** pass — **no behaviour changes, no new features, no logic edits.** If a change would
alter what a control does, it is out of scope.

### B1 — Establish one system, then apply it everywhere
The desktop already has M14's neutral monochrome ramp; `ui/theme/Color.kt` mirrors it. **Build the
phone's look on that same ramp** — do not invent a second palette.
- **Spacing scale:** 4/8/12/16/24/32 dp. One `Dimens` object; no more magic paddings.
- **Corner radii:** cards 20 dp, buttons/chips 12 dp, the mark's own 22%. Consistent everywhere.
- **Typography:** use the M3 type scale properly — `headlineSmall` for screen titles,
  `titleMedium` for card headers, `bodyMedium` for content, `labelSmall` for captions. **No
  hand-set font sizes** left behind.
- **Elevation/borders:** pick **one** (tonal surface OR a 1 dp outline) and use it for every card.
  Mixed treatments are most of why it looks unfinished.
- **Touch targets ≥ 48 dp** on every interactive element.

### B2 — Apply it to every screen, in this order
`ui/HomeScreen.kt` · `ui/ToolsScreen.kt` · `ui/SettingsScreen.kt` · `ui/StatusScreen.kt` ·
`ui/LogsScreen.kt` · `ui/OnboardingScreen.kt`.
For each: a proper top app bar with the screen name, consistent card treatment, real empty states
(icon + one sentence, never a bare "nothing here"), and **a scroll host from day one** (the M5a/M16
lesson — M16 just fixed Tools; do not let another screen ship without one).
**Keep M16's trackpad floor (`heightIn(min = 280.dp)`) and the Tools scroll host intact.**

### B3 — Do not break what M4c/M16 built
The Media, Presentation and Quick-controls cards keep their exact behaviour, their "disable, never
hide" gating and their blocked-reason text. `ToolsScreenSourceTest` must stay green — **if a check
must flip because a modifier moved, flip it and say so plainly** (the M7a lesson).

### B4 — SHOW ME, don't tell me
**Before you start**, capture `adb exec-out screencap -p` of every screen into a scratch folder
**outside the repo**. **After**, capture the same set. **In the report, list each screen with a
one-line before → after description of what actually changed visually.** Delete the captures at the
end (no stray files in the repo, GUARDRAILS) — the descriptions are the deliverable.
