## 2. Decided design

**2.1 Layout.** The right column holds a **vertical stack: tabs panel on top, Apps section beneath it.**
Apps **grows to fit its content** and the right column **scrolls** to reach it (owner's choice).

**2.2 ⚠️ THE TRAP — read this twice.** `TabsPane` contains `ListView`s (notifications, messages,
calls, shared) that scroll **internally**, which only works because they are given a **bounded**
height today. **If you simply wrap `TabsPane` in a `ScrollViewer`, it is measured with infinite
height, every list expands to its full content, and internal scrolling dies** — a serious regression
in the most-used part of the app.

**The required shape:** the right column's content is a `ScrollViewer` containing a vertical
`StackPanel` of `[TabsPane, AppsSection]`, **and `TabsPane` is given an explicit height equal to the
`ScrollViewer`'s `ViewportHeight`.** So the tabs panel is always exactly one screenful tall and keeps
its internal scrolling exactly as today; the user scrolls the column down past it to reach Apps.
Wire the height from the `ScrollViewer`'s `SizeChanged` (or `ViewportHeight` change) in
`HomePage.xaml.cs`. **Verify in your report that the notification list still scrolls within its own
bounds and did not become infinitely tall.**

*Your judgement* on the exact mechanism for keeping the height in sync, but the invariant is
non-negotiable: **`TabsPane` must never be measured with unbounded height.**

**2.3 Swap sides must still work.** `ApplyPaneOrder()` currently moves `TabsPane` between grid
columns. It must now move **the new right-column container** (tabs + Apps together) so Apps travels
with the tabs panel to the other side. Keep the M6b behaviour intact: the widgets pane keeps the
fixed width and its `MinWidth`, the tabs/Apps side keeps the star column, the splitter stays in
column 1 and still resizes the **widgets** pane in the intuitive direction.

**2.4 Apps stays a toggleable section.** Keep `apps` in `HomeLayout.AllSectionIds` and in the
**Sections** flyout — it is still a section, just one that lives on the other side. Its `Visibility`
still binds to `ViewModel.ShowApps`. **Do not change `HomeLayout`'s record shape or parameter order.**

**2.5 The grid.** Icon + name only, in a responsive grid that wraps. Use an **`ItemsRepeater` with a
`UniformGridLayout`** (`MinItemWidth` / `MinItemHeight` / spacing tuned so tiles look right at both
column widths). **Decided** — do not use `GridView` (it brings selection semantics we don't want) and
do not use a fixed-column `Grid`. Each tile: the app icon above, the label beneath, centred, label
trimmed with `TextTrimming="CharacterEllipsis"` and limited to a sensible number of lines. A tooltip
carrying the full label is fine.

**2.6 Still non-interactive.** Clicking an app does nothing until **M7**. No buttons, no hover
affordance, no selection. Keep `IsHitTestVisible="False"` or equivalent.

**2.7 Keep the existing states.** The `< v16` dimmed "older Linc app" message, the connected-but-empty
message, and the dimming approach all carry over — remember **`Border` has no `IsEnabled`** (it is not
a `Control`), which is why dimming via `Opacity` is the house pattern.
