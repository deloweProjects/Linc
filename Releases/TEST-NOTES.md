# What's new in the current test release (shown in the Telegram post; plain lines, no headings below)
• Play everywhere: fixed the PC's sound cutting out on the phone after a second (and the endless "Aligning…")
• Volume keys work while Play everywhere is on
• Phone sound on the PC now uses the mirror's sound: no Bluetooth pairing, and the phone keeps playing too
• Mirror screen keeps the sound on the phone as well
• "Now playing" on the PC works again once notification access is on
• Pictures sent from the PC show up in the phone's Gallery
• Phone Share: open the received folder, select several files, then send, share or delete them
• Sync folders: pick folders on the PC and the phone and keep them the same, both ways or one way (Control → Sync on the phone)
• Phone Terminal: long lines stay whole, swipe sideways to read them; pinch to zoom, readable title in dark
• Phone Control: a "Pages" button and an edge handle open the side panel; Mirror PC sits in the Your PC card on Home
• Phone: optional wallpaper background (Settings → Appearance)
• PC: no red banner, connection notes sit in the phone card; lighter wallpaper background; the bar no longer hides under the taskbar
• PC: Ring / Vibrate / Silent picks one; pages line up; Clipboard opens faster
• PC: the centred wallpaper has no visible edge; light theme is less white; the floating bar only rises while the taskbar is showing
• PC: the new Linc takes over from the old one in the tray (before, the old one kept running and you saw the old app)
• Phone: the wallpaper background really shows now
• Clipboard (phone and PC): search comes first; sending text to the other device is behind a "Send to PC" / "Send to phone" button
• Play everywhere can play the phone's own sound on every speaker, the phone included, in step ("Whose sound: This phone", needs USB or wireless debugging); speaker offsets, Re-sync and Smooth/Tight are under Advanced
• Phone: optional floating navigation bar, and a choice of fonts (Settings → Appearance)
• PC: Home shrinks to fit smaller windows; Now playing uses the album art; clock styles and fonts (Settings → Appearance)
• PC: Wallpaper as background can be turned off for the plain background (Settings → Appearance)
• PC: Clipboard page fits the window; Device page shows the battery; last battery and Wi-Fi stay (greyed) while the phone is away; Ring / Vibrate / Silent say when the phone needs Do Not Disturb access
