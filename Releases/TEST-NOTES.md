# What's new in the current test release (shown in the Telegram post; plain lines, no headings below)
• Mirror PC on the phone: a "Mute PC" button in the Control panel, next to Sound, so only the phone plays
• PC: new Tools page (Type, Rescue, Apps, Capture, Health, System, Commands, Console): use the phone from the PC over USB or wireless debugging, even without the Linc app running
• New connection engine (test build A, on by default; Settings › Connection on both apps turns it off): the PC and phone find each other faster on any Wi-Fi, the PC can dial the phone itself (so Windows Firewall can't block it), and a short drop or a cable pulled with Wi-Fi around doesn't show
• PC Home: "Connected over USB / Wi-Fi", plus an ADB chip that turns green when ADB is up too. If the cable works but Linc on the phone isn't running yet, it says "starting Linc on your phone" and starts it
• Pair without a cable: PC › Add a phone › Wi-Fi shows a Linc code; point the phone's camera at it and tap the link
• The phone starts Linc after a restart and when you open the app (unless you stopped it); the installer offers a firewall rule (Windows asks once)
