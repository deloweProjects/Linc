# What's new in the current test release (shown in the Telegram post; plain lines, no headings below)
• PC Home: your phone's wallpaper, sharp and full height like your lock screen, with a clock and date; widgets beside it on a soft blur of the same wallpaper
• PC: pages in a floating bar at the bottom (hover an icon to see its name); Settings › Appearance can put them back down the side
• PC: the app takes its colours from your phone's wallpaper and lets it show through, with cards that resize neatly in small windows
• Phone: Tools is now called Control; its menu opens from the ⋮ at the top right
• Control › Remote: a calmer action bar, and the keyboard sits above your phone's keyboard, with a button to bring it back
• Power shortcuts on the phone's Home
• Wi-Fi: fixes the link dropping a second after every connect
• Files: reloads by itself when the PC reconnects, and offers Try again if the PC is slow to answer
• Turning the PC screen off while mirroring: a button at the PC turns it back on
