# What's new in the current test release (shown in the Telegram post; plain lines, no headings below)
• Phone buttons beside the mirror (Device › Phone buttons beside the mirror: right, left or off): Back, Home, Recents, swipe up/down/left/right, volume, rotate, notifications, screenshot, typing, phone screen off and power, in a strip that follows the mirror window, never takes the keyboard from it and hides when it's minimised. The mirror window now keeps the picture's shape after you resize it, so there are no side bars; maximised or full screen, the bars are black or white (Bars beside the picture)
• Macros on the PC (Tools › Macros): a few steps run with one click, like Presenting (phone Do Not Disturb on, pause the music, mirror the phone). Steps: open an app or a link on the phone, a key, typed text, the PIN (asked each run), taps and swipes, any command from Commands, a screenshot, ring, Do Not Disturb, phone media, mirror, send to the phone's clipboard, open a file or app on this PC, and waits (seconds, or until an app or some text shows). Add, drag or ↑ ↓ to reorder, delete; a run shows its progress and, if a step fails, which one and why. Works without ADB for the steps that use Linc's own link
• Macros on the phone (Control › Pages › Macros): the same idea for the PC, run with ▶. Steps: PC volume, mute, brightness, media, lock, sleep, dark mode, cover the PC's screen, open something on the PC, mirror the PC, PC sound on the phone, send the clipboard or some text to the PC, open an app on the phone, the phone's Do Not Disturb, and waits. Starters: Presenting (Do Not Disturb on, PC volume 50, mirror the PC), Done presenting, Movie night, Leaving the desk
• Phone: a Linc tile in Quick Settings, lit while Linc runs and showing your PC when linked; tap to start or stop Linc
• Linc Bubble: clicking anywhere else folds it back into the bubble
• Clipboard really on its own: Android only lets Linc watch for copies after you allow "access all device logs", and it only asks while Linc is on screen. Linc now asks when you open it; allow it once and copies go both ways with no Sync button
• Linc Bubble: a click always opens its panel, with what's happening (mirror, music, a file) listed on top, each opening its card, so a mirror no longer hides the shortcuts; shortcuts keep the panel open; the pencil picks which shortcuts show (Device and Tools are new); Settings › Appearance has a size slider; the mark inside is smaller; it stays quiet while Linc is in front, and a burst of notifications pops it once
• Linc Island shows your phone's notifications: the sender (with their picture or the app's icon), the message, and its own buttons (Mark as read and the like) plus Dismiss, for a few seconds; cleared on the phone, it goes from the island too. Reply opens Linc
• More on the island: "Copied from your phone" with a peek of the text, "Pixel 7 connected" when the link comes back, and "Charging" when you plug the phone in
• Linc Bubble, your way (PC Settings › Appearance): Bubble size (Small, Medium, Large) and When the bubble is idle: a slim tab that slides into the screen edge, Hidden (nothing shows until you point at that edge or something happens), or Always a bubble. It is smaller by default, sits perfectly round with no black edge, and the tray menu has a Linc Bubble on/off switch
• Linc's own ADB is now called linc-adb.exe and keeps its own server (port 5038), so your own adb, Android Studio or other phone tools run beside it, and tools that close every adb.exe no longer knock out Linc's Mirror and Tools. (A USB cable can still only be used by one ADB at a time; wireless debugging works for both)
• Fixed: opening Linc from the desktop or Start icon while it was already running in the tray did nothing; it now brings up the window
• Linc Bubble close button: click the bubble, then ✕ (top right) to put it away; the tray menu's "Show / hide Linc Bubble" brings it back (so does Settings › Appearance)
• Linc Bubble: the bubble is the Linc mark itself, one black circle with a green rim while connected and a small dot when something is on (it was a grey disc with a ring and a blurry, off-centre icon), and smoother motion: no empty box between shapes, cards zoom out of the bubble, the bubble gives under your click and springs back, it glides into the edge, and anything that arrived while a card was open pops out once it closes
• Fixed: clipboard copies made around a Wi-Fi drop were lost both ways. A copy the PC couldn't deliver now waits for the phone, the phone sends its newest copy again when the link comes back, and if both sides copied something meanwhile the newer one wins on both
• Fixed: Mirror, Tools, app launching and phone sound with wireless debugging beside a direct link ("Could not find ADB device")
• Fixed: on the PC's hotspot (no internet) Android dropped the phone off it after a minute and stopped joining it by itself; Linc now keeps that Wi-Fi up, and gives the phone the hotspot details on every connect
• Linc Bubble rebuilt (PC Settings › Appearance › Linc Bubble): no more box around it, and it no longer slips behind other windows. It's one shape that grows out of the Linc bubble: something new (a song, a file, a call) stretches it into the island pill for a few seconds and it slides back into the bubble by itself; hover the bubble to peek; click for the card (art, progress, buttons) or, with nothing on, Linc's shortcuts. Cards close by themselves; the bubble tucks into the screen edge when idle and never steals focus from what you're doing
• Fixed: finished items (a file sent, a call ended) could stay on the Linc Island for good instead of going after a few seconds
• Fixed: "ADB online but no features". When the PC reached the phone directly and wireless debugging came up beside that link, the ADB chip lit but Mirror, screenshots, Tools, app launching and fast file copies all asked ADB for the phone under the wrong name (and on the PC hotspot couldn't even tell which ADB device was the phone). Now that ADB route is attached to the link: every ADB feature works on it, and it lets go cleanly when ADB goes
• The phone pulls its weight: when it hears the PC over Bluetooth with no link it dials the PC itself (not only "I'm here"); it keeps listening while linked, and when the PC says it has no link the phone drops its stale one at once and reconnects (no more "connected but nothing works" for a minute); with no Wi-Fi it scans at once so it joins the PC's hotspot sooner
• Automatic places: networks where the phone has linked before count as home by themselves (plus any you mark). There, with "At home, turn Bluetooth on", the PC keeps its Bluetooth on while there's no link and turns the phone's on whenever ADB reaches it. The PC always gives the phone its hotspot over the encrypted link, and starts it straight away when the phone says it has Wi-Fi on but no network (with "Start the PC hotspot when the phone is near" on); when the phone says it's on a PC hotspot, the PC dials everyone on its hotspot at once
• Sharing you can see and stop, both ways: every file moving between the PC and the phone shows with a little phone ⇄ PC animation, its live progress and a Cancel button on both Share pages, in a notification on both (Cancel right in it), and on the PC's Linc Island. The phone now shows the PC's real progress (it used to say "Sent" as soon as the file was handed over); a cancelled file is cleaned up on both sides and isn't fetched again
• Bluetooth both ways: the phone now asks for "Nearby devices" (it never did, so the PC never heard it), the PC listens again when Bluetooth is switched on later, and each side sends a small status over Bluetooth (linked, Wi-Fi, wireless debugging, hotspot). With no link, the PC's log says what the phone reports and what to try; the phone announces itself the moment it hears the PC
• Join your PC's hotspot: when the PC says over Bluetooth that its hotspot is on and the phone isn't on it, the phone shows "Join your PC's hotspot?"; one tap joins. Phone Settings › Finding your PC › Join now does it by hand. With "Start the PC hotspot when the phone is near" on, a phone with Wi-Fi on but no network gets the hotspot at once
• PC hotspot follows Windows: Linc reads Windows' real hotspot state every 5 s (turned off in Windows, or by Windows after no device used it, or turned on outside Linc), Stop really stops it, and Windows no longer switches off a hotspot Linc started while the phone is on its way
• On a PC hotspot (or any Wi-Fi with no internet) the phone no longer says "Not on Wi-Fi", and Linc turns wireless debugging back on there (it only looked at Android's main network, which stays mobile data)
• Finding your phone (PC Settings) and Finding your PC (phone Settings): Bluetooth status and what the other side last said, mark networks as home, and "at home, turn Bluetooth on" (on the PC, on the phone over the cable or wireless debugging, and the phone can ask you; both off by default). After 10 minutes with no phone the PC looks less often to save power, at full speed again the moment anything changes
• Linc Island in the app's own colours, sitting just above the phone card on Home and opening upward, with a springy pop-in and new things sliding in
• Linc Bubble (off until you turn it on, PC Settings › Appearance): a Linc bubble on the desktop over every app. Drag it anywhere, it snaps to the side and tucks into the edge when idle; it shows the island beside it when something's happening; click it for shortcuts (open Linc, mirror, ring, screenshot, send clipboard, Files, Share, PC hotspot, Connect now)
• PC hotspot by itself (PC Settings › Connection › Start the PC hotspot when the phone is near, off by default): while connected the PC gives the phone its hotspot once; later, when the PC hears the phone over Bluetooth but can't reach it, it turns the hotspot on and the phone joins by itself (Android asks once whether Linc may suggest networks). Off again after 3 minutes if the phone doesn't come
• PC hotspot (PC Settings › Connection › Start PC hotspot): when there's no shared Wi-Fi or cable, the PC turns on its own Wi-Fi for the phone, even with no internet (where Windows' own switch refuses), shows a code to join it, and finds the phone on it by itself. Uses your Windows hotspot's own name and password; leaves it on if it was already on
• Link security: PC Home and the phone's Connection sheet say whether the link is encrypted. Only plain ADB over Wi-Fi (port 5555) isn't; PC Settings › Connection › Encrypt Wi-Fi links (off by default) moves such a link to the encrypted direct one without dropping it
• Connected with ADB only: a phone with USB or wireless debugging but no Linc app now shows as connected with ADB, Files work, and Home has an Install Linc button (instead of "starting Linc on your phone…" for ever)
• Pause for banking apps (phone Settings): pick apps that refuse to open with Developer options or debugging on; while one is open Linc turns them off and back on when you leave it, even after a crash or restart
• Connection Doctor (PC Settings › Connection): checks Windows Firewall, the network type (Public/Private), VPNs, Wi-Fi isolation, ADB and the phone's battery settings, and fixes what it can
• Sent while away: links, text and clipboard copies sent while the other side wasn't connected now arrive in order when it reconnects (up to 20 items, a day at most; only the newest clipboard copy)
• Share fix: a file shared from the phone is no longer received again on every reconnect (over Wi-Fi without ADB the PC couldn't clear it from the phone, so it kept fetching it). A file that fails to arrive is tried 3 times, then Linc stops and tells you why, in the log and in a notification
• Share notifications on both sides: the PC shows each file with a live progress bar either way, then "Sent", "Received" or the reason it failed; the phone shows one for sends from Linc's own Share tab too (not only the share sheet), names the reason when one fails, and pops up "Received from your PC" (tap to open)
• PC Files: downloads over Wi-Fi without ADB no longer leave half-written files behind (which turned the next try into "name (2)"), no longer stutter on big files, and any failure shows a plain reason; "Show in folder" after a download; a reconnect reopens the folder you were in
• PC Files: Copy path puts the phone path of the selected item (or the open folder) on the clipboard
• Phone Settings › Undo changes from your PC: shows if the PC left the screen size, rotation, stay-awake, show touches, the demo-mode status bar or animation speed changed, and puts each back with one tap (Fix all for everything)
• "Fill this monitor": if the phone disconnects, its screen goes back to its own size about 5 seconds after the mirror ends (was 16)
• Phone Settings in a clearer order (what Linc can do first, then the connection), with each choice inside its own card; the Home card's chips no longer squeeze "ADB" into a column
• The PC's Apps list now shows every app on the phone (Android had been hiding many of them from Linc)
• Fewer crashes, safer saves: a failed button or drop no longer closes Linc, settings survive Linc or Windows dying mid-save, the mirror and phone-sound windows close with Linc, Quick Share no longer says "disconnected" after a send that finished, and a long Terminal session stays smooth
• ADB over Wi-Fi comes back after Linc restarts on the phone (the PC was ignoring the phone's announcements after a restart); Linc Test now looks for updates every 30 minutes
• Mirror "Fill this monitor": if the link drops, the phone now puts its own screen size and rotation back by itself after a few seconds; a "Reset screen" card on the phone and a button in PC Tools › System do it at once
• The phone and the PC now always agree on how they are connected (USB or Wi-Fi) and whether ADB is up
• ADB turns itself back on: after a restart or a Wi-Fi change Linc switches wireless debugging on again (needs the cable once, if it says so)
• Tools says "No ADB" when the phone is connected without it, and gets ADB back by itself
• Connection fixes from your test: no more endless connect-and-drop loop, the cable no longer breaks after a Wi-Fi switch, no false "install Linc?" prompt, clearer messages
• Test builds are now "Linc Test" and update themselves: when a newer test build is out, Linc Test offers it (PC restarts into it; the phone asks Android to update). No more reinstalling from Telegram
• New connection engine (on by default; Settings › Connection on both apps turns it off): the PC and phone find each other faster on any Wi-Fi, the PC can dial the phone itself, and a short drop or a cable pulled with Wi-Fi around doesn't show
• PC Home: "Connected over USB / Wi-Fi", plus an ADB chip that turns green when ADB is up too; if Linc on the phone isn't running, the PC starts it
• Pair without a cable: PC › Add a phone › Wi-Fi shows a Linc code; point the phone's camera at it and tap the link
• The phone starts Linc after a restart and when you open the app; the installer offers a firewall rule (Windows asks once)
• Mirror PC on the phone: a "Mute PC" button next to Sound. PC: the Tools page (Type, Rescue, Apps, Capture, Health, System, 331 Commands, Console)
• New look (off until you turn it on), PC Settings › Appearance › Linc Island: a dark pill at the top of your phone on Home (and in the title bar on other pages) shows a file sending or arriving, a call, music, mirroring, reconnecting or low battery; click it for buttons
• New look (off until you turn it on), phone Settings › Appearance › Frosted top bar: pages show through the top bar, blurred like frosted glass, as they scroll under it (a soft tint on Android 11)
